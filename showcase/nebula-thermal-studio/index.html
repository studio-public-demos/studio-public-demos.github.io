01 / Why this matters

Make engineering assumptions reviewable

Early electrical-panel design involves fragmented component-loss assumptions, enclosure geometry, installation environment and permissible temperatures. Spreadsheet calculations are difficult to compare, audit and communicate across design and manufacturing teams.

THE CHALLENGE

Scattered engineering data

Design constraints, device schedules, cooling assumptions and evidence are distributed across documents.

THE APPROACH

One assessment workbench

Organize enclosure conditions and heat-loss inputs for a repeatable preliminary screening result.

THE OUTCOME

Discuss trade-offs earlier

Flag possible thermal concerns, compare design options and document assumptions for engineering review.

FEATURED INTERACTION

Editable BOM and enclosure inputs

The prototype accepts a component heat-loss schedule and environmental assumptions, and recalculates its indicative assessment when those inputs change.

SCOPE SAFEGUARD

Respect sealed enclosure constraints

Ventilation alternatives are gated so an IP-rated enclosure is not casually treated as suitable for open ventilation.

05 / Professional scope

Built to show promise—without overclaiming

Demonstrated

Working private browser prototype, synthetic sample inputs, preliminary thermal screening, editable BOM, scenario views, printable output and responsive UI.

Not yet validated

No IEC 61439 compliance, IEC TR 60890 implementation, laboratory correlation, device-specific hot-spot prediction, calibrated cooling selection or connected live AI agent. Manufacturer-verified loss data are needed before professional design use.

Conceptual architecture: Engineering inputs → controlled calculation service → reviewable result → documentation. Proprietary APIs, internal design and runtime components are intentionally omitted.
