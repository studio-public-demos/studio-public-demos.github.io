Product & scenario basis

Trinity Touch publicly lists a 16-string, 1000 V DC combiner reference with a maximum 400 A switch and 26 A per-channel measurement rating. This scenario assumes each string supplies 15 A (total 240 A), two 20 A gPV fuse links per string, 6 mm² branch copper (0.4 m per polarity), 95 mm² output copper (0.55 m per polarity) at 60°C, 40°C ambient and an illustrative 55°C limit.
