# PRODUCT BRIEF: Studio Public Demos Portfolio Showcase

## Problem
The Studio Public Demos collection is a flat list of unrelated prototypes. It does not communicate Studio's breadth, product strategy, or domain expertise. Visitors see disconnected experiments instead of a coherent technology portfolio.

## Solution
A structured portfolio showcase with:
- **9 top-level categories** organized by technology domain
- **4 discovery paths**: By Category, By Technology, By Industry, Browse All
- **Classification model**: Each demo tagged with primary category, industry, capability tags, maturity
- **Search and filter** across all dimensions
- **Standardized project cards** with badges, tags, technology stack, links

## Target Users
1. **Potential customers** — evaluating Studio's capabilities in their domain
2. **Partners & investors** — understanding product strategy and depth
3. **Internal team** — navigating the demo portfolio for reference and reuse
4. **Open-source community** — discovering reusable components and approaches

## Category Architecture (9 Categories)
1. Agentic AI & Autonomous Workflows — core Studio execution capability
2. Physical AI & Engineering — simulation, CFD, computational design
3. Digital Twins & Spatial Intelligence — 3D environments, operational twins
4. GeoAI & Earth Intelligence — satellite, urban analytics, environmental
5. Computer Vision & Visual Intelligence — inspection, detection, monitoring
6. Industrial AI & Predictive Operations — factories, maintenance, IoT
7. Defence & Aerospace — dedicated sector collection
8. Climate, Environment & Sustainability — marine, emissions, circular economy
9. Enterprise Applications & Decision Intelligence — business-facing systems

## Technology Filters (9)
Agentic AI, Physical AI, Digital Twins, Computer Vision, GeoAI, Simulation, Multimodal AI, Document Intelligence, IoT & Edge AI

## Industry Filters (10)
Defence & Aerospace, Geospatial & Smart Cities, Manufacturing, Climate & Environment, Transportation, Energy & Utilities, Construction & Infrastructure, Government, Enterprise, Marine & Circular Economy

## Current State
- 38 demos cataloged across all 9 categories
- 25 active, 13 planned
- Maturity: 1 production, 7 beta, 19 prototype, 9 concept, 2 planned
- Showcase HTML built and ready for deployment
